SyncTweedies, flow edition

Re-implementation of SyncTweedies (NeurIPS 2024) as a ComfyUI sampler. Large images from overlapping views, synced at each step by averaging Tweedie estimates. Extended to flow matching with FLUX 3 Klein, and to histology with PixCell.
